#### Gradual Verification
*Summer 2025 to Summer 2026*

Gradual verification is a technique for program verification that decreases the specification burden and enables incremental development of specifications by allowing developers to write explicitly partial specifications that are backed by run-time checks.

During Summer 2025, I worked with [Long Tien Nguyen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/advancingdragon/) and [Jonathan Aldrich](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~aldrich/) on expanding the practicality of gradual verification by formalizing and proving the soundness of unfolding expressions and pure functions.

- **Preprint**. *Gradually Verifying Unfolding Expressions & Pure Functions*. Hazel Torek, Long Tien Nguyen, Priyam Gupta, Jenna DiVincenzo, and Jonathan Aldrich. [[arXiv]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15383)
- **IWACO 2025**. *Unfolding Expressions for Gradual Verification*. Hazel Torek, Long Tien Nguyen, and Jonathan Aldrich. [[Slides]](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/17FJr0gl7mJ0ldHfq-N9w-z5wXfjyYz9QxxLQtl9WC4o/edit?usp=sharing) [[Video]](https://youtu.be/RWOUvKaVN60) 
- [Poster](./assets/files/reuse25-poster.pdf) from the REUSE 2025 poster session. 

#### Metric Dimension of Infinite Graphs
*Spring 2024 to Spring 2025*

Metric dimension is a graph invariant describing the minimum number of vertices needed in a "resolving set" to uniquely identify all other vertices based on their shortest distances from those in the resolving set. Strong metric dimension strengthens this property so that non-isomorphic graphs have distinct resolving sets.

Starting in Spring 2024, I worked with [Beth Novick](https://www.clemson.edu/science/academics/departments/mathstat/about/profiles/nbeth) and [Csaba Biro](https://profiles.louisville.edu/csaba.biro) to characterize classes of infinite graphs that have finite metric dimension and finite strong metric dimension.

- **Preprint**. *Infinite Graphs with Finite Metric Dimension*. Csaba Biró, Caroline E. Boone, Beth Novick, and Hazel Torek. [[arXiv]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14405)
- **AMS Spring Southeastern Sectional Meeting 2025**. *Strong Metric Dimension of Infinite Graphs*. Hazel Torek, Beth Novick, and Csaba Biró. [[Slides]](./assets/files/ams25-slides.pdf)
- [Poster](./assets/files/honors24-poster.pdf) from the Clemson University Honors College Research Symposium in November 2024.
